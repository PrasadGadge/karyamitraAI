Missing & Incomplete Dataset Attributes

In accordance with scientific and PRAGYAN Stage 3 academic standards, the following known gaps and limitations are explicitly disclosed:

1. 100% Synthetic Source Corpus
All 60 records are synthetic test prompts. Zero production customer messages or distributor chats were collected or processed.
2. Severe Regional Vernacular Skew
55 of 60 records (91.7%) are English. Only 5 records test limited Marathi/Hinglish separator tokens (ani, pathav). No general Indic NLP is supported.
3. 0% Independent Human Reviews Completed
All 60 annotations are candidate labels created during prototype generation. An independent teammate must execute the blind review protocol before gold-standard status is established.
4. Extraction Benchmark Unevaluated
Because zero reviewed ground-truth records exist, extraction accuracy is honestly reported as "Not Evaluated" (0 eligible records evaluated).
5. No Commercial Customer Validation
No real distributor ROI, WhatsApp business webhook, voice intake, or ERP database synchronization is claimed for this prototype.
